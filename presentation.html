💡 Technical Interview Pitch Cheat Sheet

Q: How do you optimize token costs?
A: PDFs are preprocessed into clean Markdown chunks once. A local TF-IDF search engine retrieves only the top 3 relevant chunks (~1,200 tokens) per prompt.
Q: How do you link AI answers back to the PDF viewer?
A: AI responses include page numbers in citation metadata. Clicking a citation pill dispatches a page jump event to the PDF.js canvas renderer.
